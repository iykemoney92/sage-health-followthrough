Patient: Sarah Thompson
Date: 25 July 2026
Visit type: Blood pressure check and headache follow-up planning.

Context:
Sarah has been having recurring headaches and poor sleep. The GP asked her to monitor symptoms and consider a blood pressure check if headaches continued.

Clinic note:
- Blood pressure check should be booked if headaches continue beyond two weeks.
- Sarah should bring notes on headache frequency, sleep, hydration, skipped meals, and stress.
- No medication changes were made in this note.
- Sarah should seek urgent care if headaches become sudden or severe, or if she notices weakness, confusion, fainting, chest pain, or vision changes.

Useful questions for next review:
- How many headache days happened in the last two weeks?
- Were headaches worse on days with poor sleep or skipped breakfast?
- Any dizziness, visual symptoms, weakness, or fainting?
- Has Sarah been able to try short walks or hydration changes?

Follow-through needed:
- Check in every few days.
- Prepare a short two-week symptom summary.
- Remind Sarah to book a blood pressure check if headaches continue.
