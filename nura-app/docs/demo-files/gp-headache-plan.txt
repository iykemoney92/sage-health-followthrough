Patient: Sarah Thompson
Date: 25 July 2026
Reason for visit: Recurring headaches, poor sleep, feeling exhausted, and stress at work.

Summary:
Sarah reports headaches on and off for the last few weeks. She has also been sleeping badly and sometimes skips breakfast before work. No emergency symptoms were reported during this appointment.

GP advice:
- Track headaches, sleep quality, stress level, breakfast, and hydration for two weeks.
- Try a short 10-minute walk most days, ideally after dinner or during a lunch break.
- Avoid skipping breakfast where possible.
- Book a blood pressure check if headaches continue for two weeks.
- Seek urgent medical advice if headaches become sudden/severe, are associated with weakness, confusion, fainting, chest pain, vision changes, or become significantly worse.

Follow-through needed:
- Gentle check-in every few days about sleep, headaches, breakfast, hydration, and walking.
- Reminder to book a blood pressure check if headaches continue past two weeks.
- Prepare a short summary before Sarah's next GP review.
