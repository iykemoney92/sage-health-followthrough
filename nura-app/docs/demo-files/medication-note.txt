Patient: David Carter
Date: 25 July 2026
Medication: Amlodipine 5mg once daily.

Context:
David has recently started blood pressure medication. His GP asked him to take it at the same time each day and keep a simple record of how he is feeling.

GP advice:
- Take amlodipine 5mg once daily as prescribed.
- Track dizziness, ankle swelling, headaches, and unusual tiredness.
- Take a morning blood pressure reading if available.
- Book a blood pressure check in two weeks.
- Do not stop or change the dose without speaking to a clinician.
- Seek urgent help for chest pain, severe breathlessness, fainting, or symptoms that feel severe or worrying.

Follow-through needed:
- Daily medication reminder.
- Check-in after a few days about dizziness or swelling.
- Reminder to book blood pressure check in two weeks.
- Summary of readings and side effects before GP review.
