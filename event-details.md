# Build the Future of Healthcare with Juno & Anthropic

Source: https://luma.com/londonai-m2w1

## Overview

Juno is hosting the Consumer Health Hackathon at Encode Hub on July 25-26, 2026. The event is for builders who want to ship useful consumer healthcare products that help people navigate care, improve outcomes, and reduce friction in the healthcare system.

First place gets a guaranteed YC interview, sponsor credits, and bonus prizes across multiple tracks. Every attendee receives swag.

## Theme

The hackathon focuses on building AI-enabled healthcare products. Suggested directions include:

- Wearable integrations that make daily biometrics useful
- Smarter medication trackers
- Insurance copilots
- Care navigation tools
- Tools for understanding insurance, reimbursement, and healthcare paperwork
- Diagnostics, AI scribes, data integration, and other ambitious healthcare ideas

No prior healthcare experience is required.

## Sponsors and Partners

- ElevenLabs: Voice AI and audio infrastructure for building realistic speech, voice agents, and conversational products
- Supabase: The Postgres development platform powering a new generation of developer tools
- Juno (YC P26): An AI health assistant for people living with chronic illness
- Anthropic: An AI safety and research company that builds reliable, interpretable and steerable AI systems, including Claude
- OpenAI: An AI research and deployment company whose mission is to ensure that artificial general intelligence benefits all of humanity
- Vercel: Vercel is the platform for shipping websites, apps, and agents. Creators of Next.js, AI SDK, ChatSDK and Evo, the framework for building agents

## Who Should Apply

Builders, designers, engineers, and operators who want to make consumer healthcare better, especially people frustrated by broken portals, confusing bills, long phone calls, and hard-to-use healthcare products.

## Why Join

- Build something people would actually use
- Compete with serious builders
- Get hands-on with tools and infrastructure for modern healthcare products
- Leave with a real project, prizes, swag, and a shot at YC
